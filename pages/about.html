Stories That Matter, Told With Integrity

Journalism Philosophy

My approach to journalism is grounded in ethical principles, rigorous research, and a commitment to amplifying voices that need to be heard.

Truth & Accuracy

Every fact is verified through multiple sources. Every quote is accurate. Every story is grounded in verifiable truth, because credibility is journalism's most valuable currency.

Human-Centered

Behind every policy, every statistic, every headline are real people with real stories. I believe in journalism that honors human dignity and reveals larger truths through individual experiences.

Accountability

Journalism's highest calling is holding power accountable to the people it serves. I pursue stories that shine light on injustice and give voice to the voiceless.

Professional Journey

A timeline of key milestones, major stories, and career-defining moments that have shaped my approach to journalism.

2012

Career Beginnings

Started as a staff reporter at Metro Daily News, covering local government and community issues. First investigative piece exposed municipal budget irregularities.

2015

First Major Award

Won Regional Press Association's Excellence in Investigative Reporting for series on healthcare access in rural communities. Story led to policy changes.

2018

Digital Transition

Joined National Tribune as Senior Digital Correspondent, pioneering multimedia storytelling techniques and building audience engagement strategies.

2021

Independent Voice

Launched independent journalism practice, focusing on long-form investigations and collaborative reporting projects with international reach.

2025

Digital Innovation

Launched bishmabakeer.ink as a platform for immersive storytelling, combining traditional journalism with cutting-edge digital presentation.
